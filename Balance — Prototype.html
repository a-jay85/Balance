What's real, what's mocked

Worth saying in the room. It's what separates a prototype from a sales trick.

Genuinely working

  • Real  Every screen is built from your v3 handoff — the coral/navy palette, DM Serif Display, the six quest categories at their exact points values, and the locked brand line "Live well, earn points."
  • Real  State persists across reload. Close the tab mid-flow, reopen, and you land where you left off.
  • Real  What you type drives the app. Rename the family, change a child's name or age, and it propagates everywhere — including the analysis copy.
  • Real  points is one shared number. Claim a quest on Maya's phone and Sam's dashboard changes. Same state, different devices.
  • Real  Every word of the content analysis is verbatim from the case studies in the Revised MVP proposal. Nothing was written to make this demo look good.
  • Real  Goal profiles describe the week, not the child. Named for observed patterns rather than personality types, never shown to the child, and they change no access at all — consistent with the proposal's own rule that the product produces "observations of content and behaviour, not predictions about the child."
  • Real  Age drives behaviour. Under-13 triggers the COPPA verification step and suppresses the teen disclosure screen, exactly as your handoff specifies.

Mocked

  • Mocked  The analysis engine. Content is matched to a pre-analysed case; production is an LLM pipeline over transcript and metadata.
  • Mocked  Apple/Google sign-in, Apple Pay/Google Pay, and the $0.50 COPPA charge. No third party is contacted.
  • Mocked  The QR code is the decorative pattern from your handoff, not a scannable code. Network scanning and device discovery are fixtures.
  • Mocked  The backend. Nothing leaves this browser.

Not built — named as launch blockers

  • Trust-and-safety review queue and NCMEC reporting — the proposal says these ship with URL Upload, not after.
  • Editorial quality enforcement for Talking Points, and legal sign-off on liability for AI reviews of identifiable creators.
  • Format Flag thresholds — advisory board input required.
  • Crisis-line copy is US-only and needs a legal and clinical read.
  • COPPA verification method: your own note asks whether $0.50-and-refund covers the data actually collected. Still open.

Gaps I found across your own documents

  • Signup contradiction. ONB-05 in the consent PRD requires name, email, password and a mandatory ZIP. Design P-03 is one-tap SSO with no fields at all. Built to the design, per your call — but the ZIP still has to be collected somewhere for therapist matching and state rules.
  • points vs XP. The proposals and every v3 design say points. The marketing site says XP throughout. One is wrong in market.
  • No quest detail or completion flow exists in the handoff — the child screens stop at day 0. What's here is designed, not matched.
  • Teen/preteen palette. Your notes claim deeper saturation for teens; the actual tile hex values are identical in both files. Only the accent colour, greeting and subtitles differ.
  • M-04 promises a redeem CTA that isn't in the markup, and the bottom nav is defined in CSS but never placed on a child screen.
